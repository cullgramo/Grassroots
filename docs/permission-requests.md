# Permission requests (drafts)

For the owner to send. **Nothing here has been sent.** Replace the `[brackets]`. Each one asks a sports body for
permission to show its fixtures, because its site either refuses automated reading or does not publish what we need.
Drafted 19 November 2026, after the data gap analysis. The GAA key request is separate (already drafted).

The same promises go in every request, because they are how the collector already works:

- Read once a day, one page at a time, a few seconds apart, saying who we are in every request.
- Show only what a supporter needs: teams or event name, competition, age grade, date, time, ground, and your link.
- Never read or show referees', umpires' or officials' names, and nothing about players.
- No betting content of any kind.
- Credit you by name and link every fixture back to your page.
- Stop the same day if you ask, and delete what we hold.
