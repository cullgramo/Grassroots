# 10-minute phone check of the website

Do this on your own phone after a website deploy. Note anything odd with a screenshot.

1. **Home** (grassroots.expo.app): the map loads, games show near you, and the tab title reads "Grassroots: every game
   in Ireland…".
2. **List tab:** scroll, then tap a game. The fixture page shows teams, time, ground and directions.
3. **Share** a fixture to yourself on WhatsApp. The preview card shows the G, the teams, day and ground.
4. **Club:** on a fixture, tap a team name. The club page lists its games. Try "Follow".
5. **County:** open grassroots.expo.app/county/offaly (or your county). Its games are listed, and "See Offaly on the
   map" opens the map there.
6. **Add to Home Screen:**
   - iPhone: Safari, Share button, Add to Home Screen. The icon is the gold G, named "Grassroots", and it opens
     without the Safari bar.
   - Android: Chrome menu, Add to Home screen (or Install app).
7. **Share the main link** grassroots.expo.app in WhatsApp. The card shows the G and "Grassroots: every game in
   Ireland".
8. **Offline:** turn on airplane mode and reopen. The app says it can't load instead of showing a blank page.
9. **Dark mode:** switch the phone to dark mode. Everything is readable.
10. **Account:** More tab, Sign in. The emailed code arrives. This needs the email setup from `docs/accounts-setup.md`.
